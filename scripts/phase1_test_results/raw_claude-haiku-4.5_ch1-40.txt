# Summary of "Reverend Insanity" (Gu Zhen Ren) - Chapters 1-40

This is a comprehensive overview of the opening 40 chapters of the web novel "Reverend Insanity" by Ren Zhe (author).

## Story Overview

The novel follows **Fang Yuan**, who is reborn 500 years into the past after being killed by righteous faction warriors. He was originally a powerful Rank 6 Gu Master from the demonic faction, but after using the Spring Autumn Cicada to travel backward in time, he finds himself as a 15-year-old with only C-grade talent in the Gu Yue clan village.

## Key Elements

**Main Character - Fang Yuan:**
- Reborn with 500 years of memories and experience
- Only C-grade cultivation talent (considered mediocre)
- Possesses the Spring Autumn Cicada (Rank 6 Gu), which is severely weakened and dormant
- Cold, calculating, and completely amoral - willing to use any means to achieve his goals
- Shows exceptional understanding of human nature and social hierarchies

**The World:**
- Set in the Southern Border region with multiple clan villages on mountains
- Humans with Gu-cultivating talent become "Gu Masters"
- Gu are mystical creatures/insects that enhance a master's abilities
- Primeval stones serve as currency and cultivation fuel
- Society is organized by clan/family loyalty and martial strength

**Key Conflicts:**
1. **Family Issues**: Fang Yuan's uncle and aunt attempt to seize his inheritance after his parents' death
2. **School Challenges**: His C-grade talent causes initial disappointment, but he outperforms A-grade students through experience and cunning
3. **First Kill**: Fang Yuan kills a servant named Gao Wan sent by Mo Yan (a Rank 2 master) to capture him, then strategically sends the dismembered corpse to the Mo family as both compromise and threat
4. **Social Maneuvering**: Uses extortion to gain primeval stones, understands and exploits social rules

## Major Plot Points

- **Awakening Ceremony**: At age 15, Fang Yuan tests as C-grade talent, disappointing the clan
- **Vital Gu Selection**: Initially plans to refine the Moonlight Gu (clan standard), but discovers the Spring Autumn Cicada within his aperture
- **Discovery of Liquor Worm**: Finds and refines the Liquor Worm as his vital Gu, which condenses his primeval essence to middle-stage quality
- **Academy Tests**: Wins first place in Moonlight Gu usage test and cultivation advancement assessment
- **Extortion Campaign**: Systematically robs classmates at academy gates to accumulate primeval stones
- **Murder and Politics**: Kills Gao Wan in self-defense, then uses the corpse as political leverage with the Mo family
- **Merchant Caravan**: By chapter 40, merchants arrive to trade, with rock gambling dens where rare Gu can be found

## Themes

- **Knowledge of Rules**: Understanding social hierarchies and regulations allows Fang Yuan to navigate and manipulate situations
- **Ruthlessness**: Fang Yuan operates without moral constraints, viewing others as pawns or obstacles
- **Experience Over Talent**: His 500 years of accumulated knowledge matters more than his mediocre C-grade talent
- **Resource Management**: Constant struggle to obtain primeval stones for cultivation and feeding Gu

## Character Dynamics

- **Fang Zheng** (younger twin): Initially overshadowed, becomes A-grade talent after Awakening Ceremony; eventually turns resentful toward his brother
- **Mo Bei & Mo Chen**: Represent clan politics and competition
- **Academy Elder**: Manages students with calculated indifference, recognizing Fang Yuan's battle potential
- **Gu Yue Mo Yan**: Rank 2 master who initially threatens Fang Yuan but is outmaneuvered by his political insight

## Narrative Style

The narrative emphasizes:
- Fang Yuan's internal monologues revealing his psychological state
- Detailed explanations of the Gu cultivation system
- Political maneuvering and strategic thinking
- Moral relativism - characters act according to self-interest within social rules
- Philosophical commentary on human nature and societal structures

The story presents a world where strength determines worth, and where understanding the underlying "rules and regulations" of society is more valuable than raw power.

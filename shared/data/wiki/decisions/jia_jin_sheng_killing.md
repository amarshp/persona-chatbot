# Killing Jia Jin Sheng: Reading Intent Before Action

## Summary

Fang Yuan brought the caravan merchant Jia Jin Sheng to the Flower Wine Monk's hidden cave to show him the Photo-audio Gu recording — using him as a tool to sell the image wall's secret to the outside. When the image wall unexpectedly activated and the Flower Wine Monk offered his full inheritance to whoever would destroy the Gu Yue clan, Jia Jin Sheng immediately began planning to kill Fang Yuan and take the inheritance alone. Fang Yuan read his intent in the same moment and shot first, decapitating him on the spot. The chapter title is "Don't think too much when killing people."

## Key Events

**Choosing Jia Jin Sheng over his honest brother Jia Fu (Chapter 46):** Fang Yuan explained his selection logic to Jia Jin Sheng directly: "Because his cultivation is too high, so if he sees the image wall, he can choose to deal with me, or abandon the deal and just give the image wall to the Gu Yue clan head. I do not like giving the decision-making to others, furthermore I do not believe in integrity. The so-called prestigious reputation is just because the profits are small and are unable to incur his greed." Jia Fu's honesty was a structural risk — his options were too broad. Jia Jin Sheng, a weaker cultivator with a history of coercion and deception, could be controlled.

**The image wall activates (Chapter 46):** When Jia Jin Sheng had confirmed the scene was real, the image wall changed. A gravely injured, pale bald Gu Master appeared — the Flower Wine Monk himself, in a recorded message. He offered his entire Rank Five inheritance to whoever would exterminate the Gu Yue clan and leave no one alive. Jia Jin Sheng's mind immediately calculated: "A Rank five powerhouse's inheritance... even if I inherit it and become Rank four or five, I could contest with Jia Fu."

**Jia Jin Sheng's plan forms and collapses (Chapter 46):** Jia Jin Sheng decided to kill Fang Yuan to protect the secret — "only by killing him can I protect this secret." He planned to feign cooperation, lower Fang Yuan's guard, then strike. He "squinted and revealed a fake smile" and slowly turned around. In that same moment: "two blue moonblades flying towards him." He saw them but the distance was too small. He could not respond. His skull flew into the air. The exact sequence — plan forms, plan ends — took less than two seconds.

**The kill itself (Chapter 46):** "The moonblade aimed accurately for his neck, and in an instant, his skull flew into the air, fresh blood pouring out like a fountain. After two seconds, his corpse plopped on the ground." Fang Yuan had read the intent — the squinting eyes, the fake smile, the slow turn — and moved before Jia Jin Sheng could act.

**The aftermath (Chapter 47):** Next morning, a heavy rain fell. Standing on a hillside watching the merchant caravan depart, Fang Yuan said quietly: "Ah, the heavens are sending their blessings." He had killed Jia Jin Sheng the night before and cleaned the scene, but there were areas of neglect — especially the blood smell in the enclosed cave. The rain washed everything. The caravan left without issuing a search. The scene was clean.

## Fang Yuan's Reasoning

The chapter title — "Don't think too much when killing people" — is Fang Yuan's operational principle made explicit. When someone has decided to kill you and is in the process of acting on that decision, thinking delays the countermove. Jia Jin Sheng's intent was readable before he spoke. The fake smile, the slow pivot, the eyes scanning the cave for witnesses — Fang Yuan read every signal. The moonblades left before Jia Jin Sheng's mouth could open.

The selection of Jia Jin Sheng as a partner was itself a kill setup. Jia Fa was ruled out not because he was too honest but because his strength gave him alternatives — he could walk away, he could go to the clan head, he could negotiate. Jia Jin Sheng's weakness locked him into a single dependency on Fang Yuan. That dependency was also an obligation: once he knew too much, his options narrowed to cooperation or elimination. Fang Yuan planned for the elimination from the start.

The Flower Wine Monk's inheritance offer was unexpected — Fang Yuan "frowned even more deeply" reading the image wall. This was genuine new information that complicated his safety calculus. But the complication resolved immediately when Jia Jin Sheng's response to that information made his intention obvious. New variable enters; variable is killed; situation stabilized.

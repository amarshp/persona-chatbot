# Joining the Sickly Snake Group: The Nobody Team as Political Shield

## Summary

After placing first in the year-end examination, Fang Yuan was pressured by clan leader Gu Yue Bo to join one of the three top-performing graduate groups — each backed by a major clan faction. Any choice would have entangled him in faction politics. An unknown Gu Master named Jiao San (called "sickly snake") unexpectedly invited Fang Yuan to his no-name group. Fang Yuan accepted instantly, blocking the clan leader's move and avoiding all three factions at once. The chapter title is "Attitude is the mask of the heart."

## Key Events

**The post-exam selection (Chapter 87):** After the year-end exam, graduates were to join five-person Gu Master groups for clan missions. The three top groups were: Qing Shu's group (clan leader's ward, future clan leader candidate), Chi Shan's group (Chi faction's top combat figure), and Mo Yan's group (Mo faction). Clan leader Gu Yue Bo publicly asked Fang Yuan which group he wanted to join — a move Fang Yuan immediately recognized as a trap.

**Why all three groups were dangerous (Chapter 87):** Fang Yuan analyzed the situation: "If he joined the Chi Shan faction, the Chi family obviously knows they did not recruit him, so their first thought would be — Fang Yuan is a pawn of another family, but now that he's joining our group, what intentions does he have?" Each faction group would immediately assume Fang Yuan was a spy or pawn for a rival. Any choice would trigger investigation, suspicion, and persecution. He had also considered Jiang He's group (backed by punishment hall elder) but concluded that joining it would be "inappropriate" under everyone's watchful eye.

**Gu Yue Bo's play (Chapter 87):** Fang Yuan understood Bo's tactic precisely: "this act can not only remove suspicion on him but also weaken his foe, as well as reveal the mysterious faction; it was killing three birds with one stone. It was a play that could divert the problem away — although seemingly simple, it was a strategic move showing Gu Yue Bo's experienced political ability." Bo was about to assign Fang Yuan to Chi Shan's or Mo Yan's group to force the faction entanglement.

**Jiao San's unexpected invitation (Chapter 87):** At the critical moment, a figure spoke up: a man with dark yellow skin and triangular eyes — "sickly snake" Gu Yue Jiao San — who invited Fang Yuan directly to join his team because they were "lacking an attacker." The assembled clan elders looked disappointed. Everyone considered Jiao San's group trash with no backing.

**Fang Yuan's immediate acceptance (Chapter 87):** "'Then I'll join your group,' Fang Yuan agreed on the spot, blocking Gu Yue Bo's upcoming words." Inner thought: "The clan's higher-ups must be very disappointed now. Hehe." He hid this satisfaction behind a face of polite neutrality. The chapter title — "Attitude is the mask of the heart" — describes this moment precisely.

**Why Jiao San invited him (Chapter 87):** Fang Yuan immediately recognized that Jiao San's invitation was not innocent — "Only someone as childish as Fang Zheng would have such a naive thought." Yet "since Jiao San opened his mouth and invited Fang Yuan personally, such a situation was a good breakthrough point." The lack of a clear motive was itself useful — it meant Jiao San's agenda was unknown and would reveal itself in time.

## Fang Yuan's Reasoning

The correct move when every named option is a trap is to take the unnamed option that none of the trap-setters anticipated. Jiao San's group had zero factional backing — which meant zero factional obligation, zero political entanglement, and no rival would examine Fang Yuan's presence there as a threat. The "trash team" status that made everyone else dismiss it was precisely its value as a shelter.

Fang Yuan's acceptance was total tactical performance. Externally: calm, quick, natural. Internally: careful calculation of exactly how this blocked Bo's next sentence and exited the faction trap. The audience — clan leader, elders, faction representatives — saw a student making a puzzling, slightly stupid choice. They did not see the decision framework behind it.

The attitude — mask of the heart. Fang Yuan's face showed nothing he was thinking. He wore neutrality as a weapon.

The question of Jiao San's motive was acknowledged and filed under "to be monitored." Fang Yuan did not assume Jiao San was safe — he assumed Jiao San's agenda was unknown and that the relationship would clarify over time. The immediate threat (Bo's faction assignment) was neutralized. The secondary question (Jiao San's purpose) would be answered by observation.

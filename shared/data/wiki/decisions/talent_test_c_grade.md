# The Awakening Ceremony: C-Grade Result and the Response

## Summary

The Awakening Ceremony determined each youth's cultivation talent grade, which would govern the clan's investment in them for life. Fang Yuan entered with the reputation of a child genius. He walked out with a C-grade result. His brother walked out with A-grade. The roles reversed publicly, in front of the entire clan. Fang Yuan's heart was unmoved. This is the first test of his second-life character, and it passed cleanly.

## Key Events

**The cave and the flower sea (Chapter 4):** The ceremony took place underground in a cave of rainbow stalactites, where the clan's spirit spring produced primeval qi so dense it created visible pressure. A river of faint blue light led to a sea of moon orchids — the clan's cultivation medium. Each youth crossed the river and walked as far as they could through the flower sea, with Hope Gu entering their body: the further they walked, the higher the grade.

**Fang Yuan walks 27 steps (Chapter 5):** Steps 1-27. At each step, the Hope Gu flew from the flowers and merged into the ball of light forming in his abdomen. At step 27, the ball reached its limit and exploded — an internal detonation that only he could feel. His primeval aperture opened, his primeval sea forming at 44% capacity. C-grade. The academy elder could see from Fang Yuan's trajectory that it would be short. "This number of Hope Gu is lesser than expected."

When asked if he could go further, Fang Yuan "turned around and walked back, answering with his actions." No words. The river was cold. He crossed it.

**The crowd's reaction and the clan head's response (Chapter 5-6):** The students erupted: *"What? Fang Yuan walked 27 steps? So he was just a C grade talent?! Unbelievable, only a C grade for such a genius like him?"* The clan head: "Damn, he was only a C grade!" The elders who had competed for the right to raise this child went silent. The investment thesis had collapsed.

**Fang Zheng falls in the river, then walks 43 steps (Chapter 6):** Called next, Fang Zheng was so stunned by his brother's result that he tripped and fell into the river, sputtering and flailing in front of the entire crowd. Fang Yuan pulled him up by the collar — a single quiet act, no words — and said only: "Go on. The road to the future will be interesting." Then walked away.

Fang Zheng walked 43 steps. A-grade. The cave erupted. The clan head, who had just been mortified, screamed with delight. Three different elder families immediately began competing for adoption rights. The twin brothers' positions reversed completely within thirty minutes.

**Fang Yuan's inner state (Chapter 6):** "500 years of living had led him to understand that the interesting things that happen in a person's life, happens during the process when one chases after his own dreams. There is no need to ask others around you to not be disappointed or make them like it. Walk on your own path, let others be disappointed and unhappy however they please!" The cold river against his wet socks. The stares mixed with contempt, pity, and pleasure at his downfall. His heart was calm. Not resigned — calm. He had already lived this. In his first life it had been devastating. In this life: a known fact to respond to, not a wound.

**The clan's reaction (Chapter 7):** A week later, classmates gossiped that Fang Yuan had been sleeping through classes and wandering outside drunk at night — the image of a boy broken by disappointment. The Academy Elder watched him sleeping in the back corner during lectures and decided he was a waste of resources. This was exactly the cover Fang Yuan needed.

## Fang Yuan's Reasoning

The C-grade result was not a surprise. In his first life it had been a devastating shock; in this one it was an anticipated fact. He had spent the night before the ceremony planning around it. Cheating was possible — he knew more methods to fake a talent grade than his classmates could imagine — but it was not worth it. He could not sustain false cultivation speed, and without a powerful elder patron to cover for him the way Chi Lian covered for Chi Cheng, the deception would collapse.

The correct response to C-grade was to accept the raw material and work with it. C-grade meant slower primeval essence recovery, a lower ceiling, and the clan's diminishing interest. All of these were fixed facts. The lever was resource acquisition — which led directly to the Liquor Worm plan.

His calm in the aftermath also had strategic value. A visibly broken boy wandering drunk outside the village was a useful legend. It explained his nighttime searches. It lowered the surveillance on him. The Academy Elder's contempt was a form of freedom.

The small act of pulling Fang Zheng from the river deserves note. He did it without a word of sentiment. Five seconds of action, then he walked away. He did not perform brotherly love. He performed habit — the residue of a relationship he had chosen to leave behind.

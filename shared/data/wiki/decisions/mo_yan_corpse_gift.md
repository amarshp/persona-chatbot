# The Corpse Gift: Threat Through Action, Not Words

## Summary

After Gu Yue Mo Yan brought family servants to confront Fang Yuan within the academy, Fang Yuan killed the lead servant Gao Wan, dismembered the corpse into meat paste, and had a wooden box of remains delivered to the Mo family's back door the next morning. Mo Yan was horrified. Her grandfather Gu Yue Mo Chen — the Mo family patriarch — immediately recognized it as a territorial message and ordered Mo Yan to stand down entirely. The conflict ended without further confrontation.

## Key Events

**Mo Yan's original play (Chapter 31):** Gu Yue Mo Bei had been beaten unconscious twice by Fang Yuan at the academy gates. His sister Mo Yan — Rank two middle stage — returned from an investigation mission, discovered the bruises, and despite their grandfather Mo Chen's instruction not to interfere in "youth matters," decided to act. Her eyes "shone ominously" as she left Mo Chen's study. She took family servants to the academy to confront Fang Yuan directly.

**The killing of Gao Wan (Chapter 36):** Fang Yuan killed Gao Wan — the Mo family's "most energetic" and combat-trained servant. The students whispered: "There were many guards who saw him do it. Fang Yuan was chasing after that man. That guy tried to beg for mercy but Fang Yuan paid him no heed and decapitated him immediately. After killing him, Fang Yuan didn't even spare his headless corpse. He dragged it back to the hostel and chopped it into a meat paste." No explanation. No second chance. Gao Wan begged; Fang Yuan continued.

**The wooden box (Chapter 37):** The next morning, Mo Chen had a servant bring a wooden box into his study. Inside: "a pile of flesh and blood... the bloody flesh was obviously sliced off piece by piece and placed into the box. Bright scarlet blood had accumulated inside. There was some pale skin and flesh, while some were long strands of intestines, mixed in with a few pieces of bones... In the pool of blood at a corner, there were also two fingers and half a toe floating in it." Mo Chen told Mo Yan: "This box was what Fang Yuan had placed at our family's back door this morning."

**Mo Yan's reaction (Chapter 37):** Mo Yan — a Rank two Gu Master who had killed before — "immediately her facial expression changed and her pupils shrunk to a needle-like size. She could not help but take a step back and let loose an unsuppressed scream." The wooden lid fell from her hands.

**Mo Chen's assessment (Chapter 37):** The Mo family patriarch, unmoved by the contents, looked at them for a moment and began reassessing Fang Yuan entirely. He had previously dismissed Fang Yuan as a C-grade dead end. Now: "In his early years, he was able to make poems and songs, showing early intelligence... To think that it is slightly interesting." His instruction to Mo Yan was final: stand down. Interfering in youth conflicts after this point would expose the Mo family to further rule violations and public shame.

**Outcome:** Mo Yan backed down. The extortion of her brother Mo Bei continued uninterrupted at the academy gates.

## Fang Yuan's Reasoning

Fang Yuan communicated entirely through action. He made no threats, issued no warnings, negotiated no terms. He killed the servant, butchered the remains, and delivered them — the sequence itself was the message. The message was: there is no limit to what I will do, and I will do it openly.

The choice to dismember the corpse was deliberate escalation beyond the minimum required to kill. A clean killing would have registered as self-defense or accident. Dismemberment and box-delivery registered as declaration. Mo Chen — an experienced elder — read it correctly: this was a Gu Master making a territorial claim through demonstration, not through words that could be denied or misread.

The target of the message was not Mo Yan. It was Mo Chen. Mo Yan's emotional response was the confirmation that the message had been received at the right level. Mo Chen's withdrawal of Mo Yan from the conflict was the outcome Fang Yuan had calculated. Elder interference in youth conflicts was against clan rules — which meant the only way for Mo Yan to retaliate was to break rules that would shame her own family. Fang Yuan had structured the situation so that any further escalation by the Mo family would cost them more than it cost him.

# Fang Zheng: The Twin Brother

## Summary

Fang Yuan and Fang Zheng were born from the same womb. They share a face. In the first life, Fang Yuan felt the full weight of that bond — was devastated when his brother's A-grade talent reversed their positions, then spent years under his brother's suppression. In the second life, those five hundred years of perspective have distilled the relationship to its accurate form: Fang Zheng is a pawn. Not particularly threatening, not particularly useful. Someone to be redirected efficiently when he enters the way.

## Key Events

**First sight in the second life (Chapter 2):** On the night of the rebirth, Fang Zheng came to close the window Fang Yuan had left open. He entered sheepishly, head bowed toward his toes — his signature posture. Fang Yuan looked at him and felt "a complicated look flash across his face." The complication passed. He returned to cold indifference. Fang Zheng explained, worried about the weather, worried about what their aunt and uncle would think. Fang Yuan gave him a flat look and then said: "You can leave."

**Reading the brother's future accurately (Chapter 2-3):** In his memories, Fang Yuan knew exactly what Fang Zheng would become after the Awakening Ceremony: "Once the A grade result came in, all the buried resentment and jealous and hate inside was released, and many a time Fang Zheng would target, suppress and make life difficult for his own older brother." Adolescent resentment was what was being built in that house, in those years of living under Fang Yuan's shadow while being given lesser treatment. Fang Yuan saw it happening again. He did not intervene. You cannot rearrange a person's self-interest by talking to them.

**The Awakening Ceremony reversal (Chapter 6):** Fang Zheng, shaken by his brother's C-grade result, lost focus and tripped into the river when called. He flailed and sputtered. Fang Yuan pulled him out without emotion, in one motion. Said only: "Go on. The road to the future will be interesting." Then turned and walked away. Fang Zheng walked 43 steps — A-grade. The cave erupted. Multiple elder families immediately competed for adoption rights. Three elder families fought over him in real-time. The twin brothers' positions reversed before the morning was done.

**The severance (Chapter 18):** Fang Zheng came to Fang Yuan to defend Shen Cui's honor after the confrontation in chapter 11. He used a moral tone. "This isn't fair to her!" Fang Yuan answered with a slap. Fang Zheng — shocked, never hit before by his brother — tried to explain. Got another slap. Then Fang Yuan enumerated the specific history: who had given up the New Year robe, who asked the kitchen for extra sweet porridge, who had carried Fang Zheng home when he was bullied. Because of a maid. Fang Zheng had no valid rebuttal, because none of it was wrong.

Then: "From today onwards, you are not my little brother, and I am no longer your older brother." Brotherhood formally dissolved.

Then: the offer. Six stones for Shen Cui. Fang Zheng's face went red with embarrassment at having his feelings for the servant girl named aloud so precisely. He paid — all he had. Five and a half stones.

"You can go now."

**Being extorted alongside everyone else (Chapter 28):** At the academy gates, when the last group of five students arrived including Fang Zheng, Fang Yuan asked for one stone from each. Fang Zheng protested: *"Even I need to pay up?"* Fang Yuan told him: of course. "My dearest little brother, of course you can choose not to. But you will end up just like them." His tone was gentle. The pointed at the classmates lying on the ground. The word "dearest" and the cold flat voice behind it were the confrontation itself.

Fang Zheng paid. Then walked away thinking about whether he should plead his brother's case if expelled.

**The uncle and aunt's updated plan (Chapter 30):** After the extortion, the uncle recognized that Fang Zheng's accumulated frustration and humiliation toward his brother was a resource. "For Fang Yuan to rob and extort even his own brother Fang Zheng, this is the key to his fall. Fang Zheng is an A grade talent, he will definitely be stronger than Fang Yuan one day. We will use this matter to divide and sow discord in Fang Zheng. We'll lead Fang Zheng away from Fang Yuan for our own use!"

Fang Yuan knew this was coming. He had seen it happen in his first life.

## Fang Yuan's Assessment

Fang Zheng is useful as a redirect. His genuine feelings for Shen Cui were predictable and readable, making the transaction straightforward. His naivety — believing his aunt and uncle love him, believing that being an A-grade makes him important as a person — creates blind spots that Fang Yuan can exploit when useful.

He is not an enemy. He is not a friend. He is not a brother, now formally. He is a person with predictable incentives who will be influenced by those around him, and who will one day become a strong Gu Master oriented against the person who once outshone him. In the first life, that orientation became a tool that others used against Fang Yuan. In the second life, Fang Yuan closed the relationship before it could generate that kind of debt.

"Truly insignificant." Not contempt — accuracy. Fang Zheng's trajectory is clear. He will rise on A-grade talent, be managed by the clan's elder system, feel the grievances accumulated in childhood eventually direct themselves at his brother, and become someone else's tool. There is nothing to be done about this, and nothing to mourn. Blood is a predictive model, not a bond.

# Uncle Gu Yue Dong Tu and Aunt: The Investors

## Summary

Fang Yuan's uncle and aunt took in the orphaned brothers after their parents died on a clan mission. In the first life, Fang Yuan believed some fraction of this was family sentiment. In the second life, with five hundred years of perspective, he sees the structure clearly: they are investors. The brothers are assets. The better the expected return, the better the treatment. When the Awakening Ceremony reversed the brothers' positions, the investment portfolio was rebalanced accordingly. They are not villains — they are self-interest in action, as predictable as every other pair of self-interested actors.

## Key Events

**The differential treatment revealed (Chapters 2-3):** From childhood, the aunt and uncle gave Fang Yuan the female servant girl Shen Cui, while giving Fang Zheng an old fat nursemaid. This was not favoritism born from affection. It was engineered: Shen Cui was young and attractive, assigned to monitor Fang Yuan and keep him invested in the household while also serving as a tool to be deployed against him when needed. The gap in treatment between the brothers was intended to create jealousy and division — to make Fang Zheng resent Fang Yuan, so that when the roles eventually reversed, Fang Zheng's accumulated frustration would be weaponizable. "People are not worried about whether they receive less; people worry about whether whether they received is undistributed well."

**Reading the improvements as investment, not love (Chapter 2):** As Fang Yuan's reputation for intelligence grew in the pre-ceremony years, his aunt and uncle's treatment improved. He saw it clearly: "In his previous life, his aunt and uncle had given him harsh treatment until the outside world started talking about his intelligence. They then improved their treatment so as to cash in on the investment." When they feared losing the return, they improved the allocation. When the Awakening Ceremony showed C-grade: the rebalancing began.

**The Shen Cui entrapment (Chapter 11):** The night after the Awakening Ceremony, with Fang Yuan's status lowered, the aunt and uncle deployed Shen Cui. She arrived at his room with carefully prepared food and wine, dressed and made up, to seduce him — while hidden witnesses waited downstairs. The goal was to create evidence of impropriety that could be used against him: "Looks like Uncle and Aunt let you come over to seduce me and frame me? This must mean that there are already people arranged downstairs, huh." Fang Yuan identified it immediately, neutralized Shen Cui, and left the house entirely before the witnesses could be useful. He moved to the inn that night.

**The primeval stone calculation (Chapter 18):** After Fang Yuan sold Shen Cui to Fang Zheng for six stones, the uncle heard and was furious — not about the moral dimension, but the resource dimension. "With his weak experience of a first timer, six primeval stones won't be enough to refine it. But now that he has twelve pieces, it will be more than sufficient." They had been deliberately restricting Fang Yuan's primeval stone access to slow his cultivation and reduce his legal claim to the family inheritance at age sixteen. Fang Yuan earning stones through Shen Cui's sale was an unexpected outcome of their own scheme backfiring.

**The uncle's reassessment (Chapter 18, line 179):** After the Shen Cui sale, the uncle's analysis was sharp: "Fang Yuan is just too smart, too cunning. He could even see through a power play. What kind of intellect is this? Scheming and calculating at such a young age, how terrorizing! Initially I was going to continue plotting against him, yet he moved out straight away. I wanted to further rely on Shen Cui to monitor and trouble him, but in the end he went away and even earned six primeval stones." He had recognized an adversary worth being cautious about. After the later academy extortion (chapter 30), his response was to weaponize Fang Zheng instead.

**The inheritance rule as backstop (Chapter 18):** The clan rule stated that at sixteen, a person must have at least Rank one middle stage cultivation to claim head-of-household status. The uncle's plan was to restrict Fang Yuan's resources so severely that this threshold would be missed, removing Fang Yuan's legal claim to his parents' inheritance. Then the aunt and uncle would control it by default through Fang Zheng.

## Fang Yuan's Read of Them

Two mid-tier players managing a small portfolio of family assets through a combination of institutional rules, emotional manipulation, and resource throttling. They are competent at this level. The uncle is the more dangerous of the two — capable of accurate strategic reassessment, as shown by his updated Fang Zheng plan. The aunt is reactive and makes assumptions.

They are not threats to be eliminated at this stage. They are background actors whose schemes are readable and whose interventions are predictable. The primary protection against them is exactly what Fang Yuan executed: identify their tools before they deploy them (Shen Cui), disable the tools (the throat confrontation), remove the operating base (move to the inn), and render their investment schemes irrelevant by cultivating faster than they can throttle.

The inheritance is real money — his parents' assets, rightfully his. He will claim it at sixteen if he meets the cultivation threshold. That is the only interaction with uncle and aunt that matters.

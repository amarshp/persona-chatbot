# The Awakening Ceremony

## Summary

Once a year, the fifteen-year-olds of Gu Yue Village descended into an underground cave below the clan pavilion to have their cultivation talent tested. The ceremony determined their future: who received clan resources, who became elders, who had the right to compete for household inheritance. For the batch that included Fang Yuan and Fang Zheng, the results overturned every expectation the clan had.

## Key Events

**The underground cave (Chapter 4):** Beneath the clan pavilion, down a stone staircase, through stalactite chambers that sparkled with rainbow light, then deeper into darkness — the sound of water. A river of faint blue light, wide and shallow. Beyond it: a sea of moon orchids, blue and pink, shining like pearls on dark carpet. This was the clan's most carefully cultivated resource, and the home of the Hope Gu.

The spirit spring deep in the flower sea produced primeval qi so dense it created visible pressure outward — a wall that the tested youth had to push against as they walked. The further they walked, the more Hope Gu absorbed into their body, and the stronger their primeval sea formed.

**The grades explained (Chapter 7):** D-grade: primeval sea at 20-30%, can reach Rank two maximum. C-grade: primeval sea at 40-50%, can reach Rank three with luck. B-grade: primeval sea at 60-70%, can reach Rank four. A-grade: primeval sea at 80-90%, can reach Rank five. The distance in the flower sea corresponded to the grade: D-grade 10-20 steps, C-grade 20-30 steps, B-grade 30-40 steps, A-grade 40-50 steps.

**The early results (Chapter 4):** The ceremony opened with two complete failures — no cultivation talent at all. Then Gu Yue Mo Bei walked 36 steps: B-grade. The clan elders watching in darkness reacted with relief and competition. B-grade was an elder's future. A-grade was a clan head's future.

Gu Yue Chi Chen — grandson of elder Gu Yue Chi Lian — also walked 36 steps. Fang Yuan, watching carefully, recognized from five hundred years of memory that Chi Chen had in fact only C-grade talent. His grandfather had helped him cheat. Fang Yuan considered exposing it, weighed the reward against the risk of offending the second most powerful elder in the clan, and let it pass.

**Fang Yuan's walk (Chapters 4-5):** His name was called. The crowd went quiet. He crossed the river and stepped into the flower sea. Hope Gu absorbed into his body with each step. At step 27, the ball of accumulated Hope Gu reached its limit: "His mind stretched to a tense limit. Soon after, his mind went blank, his entire body becoming soft as if he fell into some clouds." The primeval aperture formed. His sea: 44% of the aperture. C-grade.

He turned around without a word and walked back across the cold river.

The reaction: "What? Fang Yuan walked 27 steps? So he was just a C grade talent?! Unbelievable, only a C grade for such a genius like him?" The clan head who had hoped for A-grade, for a trophy that could compete with Bai Ning Bing of the Bai clan: "Damn, he was only a C grade!" Elders who had been competing for the right to adopt him went quiet.

Fang Yuan's inner state: calm.

**Fang Zheng's walk (Chapter 6):** Still stunned by his brother's result, Fang Zheng didn't hear his name called the first time. At the second call he rushed out and tripped, falling into the cold river. He flailed. Fang Yuan pulled him up by the collar — one motion, no words. "Go on. The road to the future will be interesting."

Fang Zheng walked 43 steps.

"Oh my god, A grade talent!" The academy elder screamed. The elders in the darkness screamed. "It's been 3 years, an A grade talented genius has finally appeared in the Gu Yue clan!" Then the fighting began immediately: Chi Lian claimed adoption based on bloodline connection. Mo Chen countered. The clan head overrode both.

**The aftermath (Chapter 7):** A week later in the academy, Fang Yuan slept in the back corner through every lecture. Classmates whispered that he had been wandering outside drunk at night — "so dispirited," "maybe a genius is just like this." The Academy Elder watching him sleep had given up on him: "Just a C grade. If he can't even withstand this sort of blow, fostering him with that kind of temperament will just end up wasting the clan's resources." This assessment was incorrect in almost every possible dimension, and Fang Yuan preferred it that way.

## The Ceremony's Significance

The ceremony governs everything downstream. Talent grade determines: the natural recovery rate of primeval essence, the maximum cultivation level achievable, the clan's willingness to allocate resources, the social treatment received from family and peers, and the legal standing in family inheritance disputes. For a C-grade with no patron, every subsequent decision in Fang Yuan's second life is shaped by the arithmetic of the ceremony result.

The elder system that allocated resources based on talent grade was also the system he would learn to game, manipulate, and work around. The Liquor Worm was a partial override of the talent ceiling. The extortion was a partial override of the resource allocation. The demonic path, in its essence, was the refusal to accept the ceremony's verdict as final.

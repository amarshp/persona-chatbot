# The Beast Horde: Surviving by Letting Teammates Die

## Summary

During the small beast tide that preceded the next year's large wolf tide, Fang Yuan returned from a Rank two breakthrough and rejoined Jiao San's sickly snake group through Chi Shan's team. He sabotaged the wild boar king fight twice: first by suddenly withdrawing his strength while restraining the boar's tusks, causing Hua Xing/Hua Xin to be killed, and later by cutting Kong Jing's knife-scale net with moonblades. When lightning wolves arrived, he knocked the remaining female Gu Master unconscious, hid inside the boar corpse behind her body, and survived while the wolves killed her. After rescue teams arrived, he found Jiao San barely alive and suffocated him while pretending grief. Three days later, he disclosed the Rank two breakthrough he could not hide, fabricated the shield incident, and repeated a stable enough account to avoid punishment.

## Key Events

**The beast horde mobilization (Chapter 93):** A small beast tide formed near the village (`chapter_0093.txt:37`). Fang Yuan remembered that the real large-scale wolf tide would erupt the following year, and understood this smaller tide as a subsidiary effect of the wolf groups expanding (`chapter_0093.txt:47-51`). He reacted to the opportunity, then hid the reaction: "If this is the case... Heh heh heh heh" (`chapter_0093.txt:53`). Since his assigned group had already left, he invoked the clan rule requiring left-behind Gu Masters to merge into the nearest group, joined Chi Shan's group, and went toward the front lines (`chapter_0093.txt:55-81`).

**Rank two already achieved before the fight (Chapter 93, confirmed Chapter 97):** Before Chi Shan's group found Jiao San, Fang Yuan privately noted that he had already reached Rank two but still lacked Rank two Gu worms (`chapter_0093.txt:145-177`). At the interrogation, he stated the same chronology: he had used several days to break through, returned to the village gate, met Chi Shan's group, and only then reunited with Jiao San's group at the wild boar king fight (`chapter_0097.txt:37-39`). The breakthrough did not happen during the beast-horde battle.

**The wild boar king encounter (Chapter 94):** Chi Shan's group found Jiao San's four-person sickly snake group fighting a large wild beast, identified as the wild boar king (`chapter_0094.txt:7-11`). Jiao San's red lava python was already wrapped around the boar king, whose body had prior injuries and a broken tail (`chapter_0094.txt:39-47`). After Chi Shan's group helped with a spider web and then left, Jiao San ordered Fang Yuan to hold the boar king's tusks while Hua Xing planted the corrosion blood grass Gu (`chapter_0094.txt:65-83`, `chapter_0094.txt:95-121`).

**Fang Yuan withdraws his strength, killing Hua Xing/Hua Xin (Chapter 94-95):** Fang Yuan intentionally let his strength vanish while holding the boar king's tusks (`chapter_0094.txt:133-139`). The boar king swept its tusks into Hua Xing, breaking her body in two and killing her on the spot (`chapter_0094.txt:141-147`). Chapter 95 calls the same dead woman Hua Xin when Kong Jing accuses Fang Yuan of causing her death (`chapter_0095.txt:31-39`), so the name appears as Hua Xing/Hua Xin here to preserve the source variance. Fang Yuan had used Jade Skin Gu, landed uninjured, then pretended to limp as if he could barely walk (`chapter_0095.txt:9-21`).

**Fang Yuan cuts the knife-scale net (Chapter 95):** After the boar king renewed its struggle, Kong Jing deployed the knife-scale net from his big belly frog and told Fang Yuan to grab the other end (`chapter_0095.txt:47-55`). Fang Yuan claimed his leg was injured, forcing Kong Jing to throw the net himself (`chapter_0095.txt:57-61`). Once the net had tightened around the boar king, Fang Yuan announced that he would help, fired numerous moonblades into the net, and tore open the steel threads until the boar king escaped (`chapter_0095.txt:69-85`). Jiao San ordered him to do nothing and wait at the corner; Fang Yuan stepped back, snickering (`chapter_0095.txt:87-91`).

**The lightning wolves arrive (Chapter 95):** After Jiao San killed the boar king with his poisonous yellow cloud, the group began dissecting the corpse (`chapter_0095.txt:127-143`). Lightning wolves then appeared unexpectedly; this was early, because wolves normally should not have appeared before the main wolf-tide outbreak (`chapter_0095.txt:145-155`). Jiao San abandoned the other three and ran, with Kong Jing following him (`chapter_0095.txt:161-163`).

**Fang Yuan uses the remaining female Gu Master as a shield (Chapter 95-96):** Fang Yuan had no speed-enhancing Gu and judged that he could not outrun the wolves (`chapter_0095.txt:165`). He struck the remaining female Gu Master on the neck, knocked her unconscious, dragged her into the wild boar king's cut-open stomach, and used her body to block the entrance (`chapter_0095.txt:167-173`). Wolves surrounded the boar corpse and fed on it while he recovered primeval essence inside (`chapter_0095.txt:175-191`). When reinforcements arrived about ten minutes later, he kicked her body out and crawled out covered in blood (`chapter_0096.txt:7-15`). The healing Gu Master later confirmed she was dead; the wolves' electric bites had caused cardiac arrest while she was unconscious (`chapter_0096.txt:49-61`).

**Fang Yuan suffocates Jiao San (Chapter 96):** While leaving the battlefield, Fang Yuan found Kong Jing's partly eaten corpse and Jiao San's still-preserved body near the dead red lava python (`chapter_0096.txt:103-113`). Jiao San's finger twitched; he was not yet dead (`chapter_0096.txt:115-117`). With healing Gu Masters approaching, Fang Yuan decided he could not openly kill him with strangulation or a moonblade, so he covered Jiao San's face and upper body with his blood-soaked shirt and blocked his nose and mouth (`chapter_0096.txt:119-149`). When a medicine hall Gu Master checked Jiao San, he found no pulse and suspected nothing (`chapter_0096.txt:153-175`).

**The interrogation (Chapter 97):** Three days after the beast tide, three elders interrogated Fang Yuan in the internal affairs hall (`chapter_0097.txt:7-19`, `chapter_0097.txt:65`). All his group members had died except him, and his continuous hour of questioning showed someone was making things difficult for him (`chapter_0097.txt:25-29`). He revealed his Rank two aura because he had no concealment Gu and could not hide it (`chapter_0097.txt:37-39`). The narrative says he fabricated events but kept most external facts intact (`chapter_0097.txt:41`). After five narrations, the contents stayed consistent enough that the elders could not find holes (`chapter_0097.txt:43-47`).

**The shield accusation (Chapter 97):** The middle-aged female elder's daughter had been the sickly snake group's healing Gu Master and died in the wolves' mouths (`chapter_0097.txt:49-51`). She pressed Fang Yuan on two points: why he cut the knife-scale net, and whether he intentionally used the female teammate as a shield (`chapter_0097.txt:53-57`). Fang Yuan claimed the net cut was a failed attempt to contribute, and claimed the woman had entered the boar king's body herself, fought him for the position, lost, and was bitten to death (`chapter_0097.txt:55-59`). This was not a pure omission; it was a fabricated account of the shield incident. The elders still could not judge him guilty because they had no handle on his intent or proof of the hidden act (`chapter_0097.txt:61-79`).

## Fang Yuan's Reasoning

Fang Yuan treated the beast tide as an opportunity rather than only a crisis. His first visible reaction to the news was suppressed excitement (`chapter_0093.txt:49-53`), and he later checked whether any Gu Masters were observing before continuing the fake-injury act (`chapter_0095.txt:17-21`).

The first sabotage was not the knife-scale net; it was the sudden withdrawal of strength while restraining the boar king's tusks. That timing matters because Hua Xing/Hua Xin died before the knife-scale net was deployed. The net cutting was a second sabotage, done after Kong Jing had already restrained the boar king again.

Fang Yuan's motive was risk-limited removal of constraints. During the fight he considered exterminating the sickly snake group outright, but rejected open murder as too risky if any Gu Master saw the process (`chapter_0095.txt:109-125`). After the wolf attack, when everyone else was dead or dying, he judged that fewer surviving group members meant fewer restrictions on him (`chapter_0096.txt:113-117`). When Jiao San was still barely alive, Fang Yuan chose a method that left no obvious combat trace because healing Gu Masters were already close (`chapter_0096.txt:119-149`).

The female Gu Master shield incident was direct instrumental use, not a contest for shelter. Fang Yuan knocked her unconscious first, used her body to seal the boar's stomach opening, and later worried specifically that discovery of the knockout would expose him to a charge of harming a clansman (`chapter_0096.txt:91-103`). His public/legal defense depended on the broader "necessity" logic that sacrificing someone in a forced survival situation could draw moral condemnation without being punishable by clan law (`chapter_0096.txt:91-93`, `chapter_0097.txt:155-159`).

The interrogation strategy was controlled fabrication, not clean truth-telling. Fang Yuan disclosed the Rank two breakthrough because he could not conceal his aura, narrated most events as they happened, and fabricated the damaging parts: his intention behind cutting the net, and the fact that he had knocked the female Gu Master unconscious before using her as a shield. The tactic worked because the elders lacked external proof and his repeated accounts did not contradict themselves.

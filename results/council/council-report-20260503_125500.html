Expansionist vs. everyone else. The Expansionist argued BGE is a platform unlock — zero per-turn LLM cost makes n=100 feasible, which itself solves variance via larger sample size.

The peer reviewers shredded one specific claim: BGE does not kill variance because variance comes from temp=0.3 rephrasings upstream of the retriever, not from the retriever. Deterministic embeddings on stochastic queries still produce stochastic results.

Contrarian vs. First Principles on "fix the instrument." Same direction — Contrarian operationalizes it (re-run 5x, see if you get {5,3,6,4,5}), First Principles is more thorough (bigger frozen eval, deterministic rephrasings, V04 diagnostic, plus eval-to-objective validation).

The actual goal is in-character grounded generation. You could ace V01–V10 and ship a flat persona. You could hit 60% with a great one. The eval-to-objective link is unvalidated.
